Source: 3:20.02 · 14 consecutive segments · Maximum interval: 15 seconds

Current screens were inspected at five-second intervals. Thumbnails reference the existing video. Narration is proposed replacement wording, not a transcript; confirm pacing with a read-through. The final endpoint is rounded up to cover the last fraction of a second.

JMS’s feedback: Make the benefits of each route explicit for students and connect the choice to instructor goals. Keep the AI directions concise.

Revision strategy: Replace extended scrolling and navigation with concise benefit cards: AI offers candidates to evaluate; non-AI develops discovery through direct research. Both produce evidence of research judgment and understanding under a shared rubric. These are intended benefits, not measured results.
